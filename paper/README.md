# The CPO paper, as far as the course has reached

*CPO: a modular ontology for lighting information in building projects* is the worked example of TADS23, written
with the course's report template. Use it as an example of what your own report can look like.

This version goes as far as Lecture 8: the introduction, the literature review, the method, the ontology with its
modules, the composed ontology and its spanning axioms, the review, publication and the first tests (ABID-Mo steps
1 to 10, Lectures 4a to 4c); and the information delivery cycle run on it (Section 5): the IFC model converted to a
graph (Lecture 5), the competency questions answered with SPARQL in GraphDB (Lecture 6, ABID-Mo step 11), the
classification with Uniclass and IEC 81346-2 and the level of information need (Lecture 7), and the SHACL validation
and the SWRL rules (Lecture 8). The Discussion and the Conclusions are added when the course reaches them, after the
analysis of CQ7 and the return of the results to Revit.
